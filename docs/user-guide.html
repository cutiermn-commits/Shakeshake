Your guide

Shake Shake 🍳

The only shake you need is the one you give the pan.

Shake Shake helps you eat more protein from real South Indian food instead of ready-made protein shakes. It suggests what to cook for lunch and dinner, plans your week, writes your grocery list, and estimates calories and protein from a photo of your plate. Friends can cheer you on, and the app sends the occasional sweet, funny nudge along the way.

Getting started (about 5 minutes)

  1. Open the app on your iPhone in Safari

    Tap the invite link a friend sent you, or open the link above.

  2. Enter your email, then the code

    Tap Send me a code. Check your email (and spam) for a 6-digit code and type it in. No password needed, ever.

  3. New here? Add your invite code

    If the app asks for one, it's a short code of letters and numbers like K7QM4XPA. The friend who invited you can make one in Me → Friends → Create invite link. If you opened their link, it's filled in for you.

  4. Set yourself up

    Five quick screens: your name and avatar, body and goal, food you like (and allergies), your daily targets, and your privacy choices. The app suggests calorie and protein ranges; you can change them.

  5. Add it to your Home Screen

    In Safari tap Share ⬆️ → Add to Home Screen. From now on, open Shake Shake from its icon.

  6. Turn on reminders

    Open the app from the Home Screen and tap Turn on reminders on the Today screen. iPhone only allows this for Home Screen apps (iOS 16.4 or later).

A normal day

🍛 Pick lunch and dinner (Today)

  • Tap Suggest lunch. A recipe idea appears in about 30 seconds.
  • Tap Swap for three alternatives: more protein, faster, different protein or veg tonight.
  • Tap Recipe to see ingredients, steps and the protein per serving.
  • Tap Choose when you've decided. You'll get a "show me your plate" reminder about 30 minutes after your usual meal time.

📸 Log what you actually ate (Log)

  • Take a photo of the plate (plate only, no faces). Location data is removed before it's saved.
  • Tap ✨ Estimate for a calorie and protein range, or just type the numbers yourself.
  • Fix anything that looks off, then tap Save meal. Tick Keep this meal private 🔒 if friends shouldn't see it.

📈 Add your burn (Me)

  • Type today's active calories from your Oura app (or any tracker) and tap Save.
  • See eaten vs burned, protein vs target, and a 7-day view. Numbers marked "estimated" are approximate.

Once a week

  • Plan tab → ✨ Plan my week fills 7 lunches and 7 dinners (no dish more than twice). Open any day to swap or choose a meal, and set how many people you're cooking for.
  • Tap 🛒 Grocery list for this week. Items are grouped by aisle (meat & fish, dairy, pulses, vegetables…). Tick things off as you shop, show or hide pantry staples, and tap Share 📤 to send it on WhatsApp.

Friends & the leaderboard

Invite friends from Me → Friends → Create invite link, or swap 8-letter friend codes. You'll see each other's shared meals in the Friends tab, where you can react, comment, or report anything unkind.

Points are a percentage of your own goals, so everyone competes fairly whatever their size:

up to 200🏃 1 point per 1% of your active-calorie goal
up to 100💪 1 point per 1% of your protein target
up to 50📸 20 per photo-logged meal (2 a day) + 10 for a streak

Up to 350 points a day. The board resets every Monday at midnight (India time), and goals are locked for the week.

What the labels on the numbers mean

Calculated

Worked out from a public food database (USDA FoodData Central) using the recipe's raw ingredient weights.

≈ Partly calculated

Some ingredients aren't in the database (for example paneer or curry leaves), so it's approximate.

AI estimate

Mostly the AI's guess. Treat it as a rough idea.

Your privacy, your choice

  • Each choice is a separate switch in Me → ⚙️ Settings: storing health details, AI help, sharing meals with friends, and reminders.
  • AI help uses Google Gemini on its free tier. Google says content sent on the free tier may be used to improve its products. We never send your name or email. Turn AI off any time; typing meals in yourself always works.
  • Your weight, age, height and allergies are never shown to friends.
  • Download all your data or delete your account (and photos) from Settings. Plate photos are deleted automatically after 90 days.

Quick help

I didn't get the code

Wait a minute and check your spam folder. On the code screen, tap Send a new code. Make sure the email is spelled correctly (tap Change email).

It says I need an invite code

That email isn't a member yet. Ask the friend who invited you to send an invite link from Me → Friends; opening it fills in the code.

Suggestions say AI is off

Go to Me → ⚙️ Settings → Privacy choices and switch on AI help. You can still log meals by hand without it.

The idea is taking a while

A new recipe can take up to about 30 seconds. If the AI is busy or has reached its daily limit, the app tells you and offers a saved recipe instead.

I'm not getting reminders

Open Shake Shake from its Home Screen icon (not Safari) and tap Turn on reminders in Settings. Reminders don't arrive between 10 pm and 7 am, and anything beyond 4 a day waits in your 💌 messages.

The calorie number looks wrong

Photo estimates are approximate. Correct the numbers before saving; you can delete a meal later from the Me tab.
