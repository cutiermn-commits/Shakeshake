Shake Shake

Design choices that matter

AI never stops the app

Three layers: Qwen on Groq first, then Gemini, then a library of 120 hand-written recipes stored in the database. Manual logging always works, even with AI switched off.

The AI doesn't do the maths

The AI writes the recipe. The app works out calories and protein from a local USDA FoodData Central table, and shows a badge saying whether the numbers are calculated, partly calculated or an AI estimate.

Privacy built in

  • Row Level Security on every table: you can only read your own rows.
  • AI use needs a separate, revocable opt-in.
  • Photos are shrunk and stripped of location before upload.
  • Account export and delete are built in.
  • Keys stay on the server, never in the phone.

Kind by default

The message engine caps push notifications at 4 a day, respects quiet hours (22:00–07:00) and limits love notes to 2 a week. If the last few days' eating looks too restrictive, safety mode replaces every playful line with a calm check-in.
